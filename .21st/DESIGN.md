# Titan Autoworx design context

The visual system is a premium motorsport workshop: carbon black, titanium silver, and Titan red. Large condensed italic headlines should feel fast and engineered. Photography is cinematic but physically believable, with controlled red practical light and deep black surfaces. Motion should reward attention without delaying content; reduced-motion users receive the complete static composition.

Avoid orange accents, generic glassmorphism, soft lifestyle styling, excessive pill shapes, and sci-fi interface decoration. The brand should feel credible to everyday repair customers and aspirational to performance and restoration clients.
